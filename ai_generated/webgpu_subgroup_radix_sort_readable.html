Subgroup radix sort with 8-bit digits

Two stable passes over x (a 16-bit key), 256 bins per pass. Workgroup size and shared-memory tables are sized from the GPU’s reported subgroup range, so laptop GPUs with small or variable subgroups still fit. y is the original index, and a GPU check confirms order and stability.

Chrome rounds timestamps to 100 µs unless chrome://flags/#enable-webgpu-developer-features is on.
